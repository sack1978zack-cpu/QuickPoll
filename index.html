Step-By-Step Guide

How to Use QuickPoll

Create, customize, share, and track live polls in under 30 seconds. Follow the steps below to launch your first poll or embed QuickPoll on your website.

STEP 01

Write Your Question

Open the Question page, type your prompt, or tap an inspiration starter. Watch the live readiness meter and real-time preview update on every keystroke, and pick your favorite Gold, Black, Blue, Purple, and Red gradient style.

STEP 02

Add Answers & Expiry

Move to the Answers page to add between 2 and 8 choices, reorder them anytime, or enable multiple picks. Choose a quick expiration preset or set an exact date and time for automatic closing.

STEP 03

Share the Unique Link

Click Generate Unique Poll Page to create your shareable poll URL. Send it to friends via chat or social apps. Anyone opening the link sees the dedicated poll page with a live second-by-second countdown.

STEP 04

Watch Live Results & Add Icons

When friends vote, animated gradient bars highlight the leading option in real time. Once the countdown hits zero, voting locks automatically. You can also place your own transparent PNG or SVG icons into any marked icon slot in the HTML.
