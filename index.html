How to Use QuickPoll

Four steps from question to live results.

01

Enter Question & Answers

Write your question and 2 to 8 answers. Choose single or multiple picks and watch the live preview update.

02

Pick an Expiry Time

Select a preset duration or set an exact date and time. Voting closes automatically when the timer ends.

03

Send the Unique Link

Click Generate Poll Link and send the URL to friends. Every poll opens on its own clean voting page.

04

Track Live Results

Reopen any poll anytime from My Polls to check live tallies, remaining time, or copy the link again.

Privacy Policy

How poll data, browser storage, and ads work on QuickPoll.

1. Poll Data & Local Storage

QuickPoll does not require accounts, names, or email addresses. Polls you create are either encoded directly inside the shareable URL or saved on the host server solely to display the poll and count anonymous votes. Your browser's localStorage stores your created poll history and prevents duplicate votes from the same browser.

2. Shared Links

Anyone with a poll's unique link can view that poll's question, answer options, expiration time, and total vote counts. Avoid including private or sensitive personal details inside poll questions or answers.

3. Cookies & Google AdSense

This website uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to visitors based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com) or by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Managing Your Data

You can delete your saved polls anytime from My Polls or by clearing your browser site data.
