Privacy Policy

1. Poll Data & Browser Storage

QuickPoll does not require accounts, names, or email addresses. Polls you create are either carried inside the shareable URL or saved on the host server solely to display the poll and count anonymous votes. Your browser stores your created poll list and vote lock locally.

2. Shared Links

Anyone with a poll link or QR code can view its question, options, expiration time, and vote totals. Avoid placing private personal information inside polls.

3. Cookies & Google AdSense

This site uses Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites across the Internet.

Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings or at aboutads.info.

4. Managing Data

You can remove saved polls anytime from My Polls or clear your browser site data.
